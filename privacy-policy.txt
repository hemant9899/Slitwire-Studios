# Privacy Policy for Solej AI

**Last Updated: October 13, 2026**

## Overview

Solej AI ("the App") finds stories in your photo library and lets you search your photos. All photo processing happens on your device. Your photos, search text and results are never uploaded to any server.

## What Never Leaves Your Phone

- Your photos and videos
- What the App finds in them: stories, labels, recognised text and search indexes
- Your searches
- Your location, contacts or any other personal data

## What Does Leave Your Phone

### Model downloads
After install, the App downloads its on-device models once from the model host (Hugging Face). Like any download, the host sees a standard web request from your device (for example its IP address). No photos or personal data are sent.

### Crash reports (on by default, you can turn them off)
If the App crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. A report contains the error type, a random install identifier and device state (device model, OS version, free memory). It never contains photos, names, searches or text from your photos. Turn crash reports off any time in Settings > Privacy.

### Text recognition on Android
On Android, the on-device text-recognition component (Google ML Kit) sends usage statistics to Google, such as performance and error counts. It never sends photos or the text it recognises.

### Subscriptions
Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) processes your payment. We never receive your payment details, card number or billing address. The App receives a confirmation from Apple or Google to unlock Premium on your device.

## Camera and Photo Library Access

- **Photo Library**: used to find stories in your photos and to search them. Limited Access works. Photos are processed on your device and never transmitted.
- **Camera**: used only when you choose to take a photo. The photo is processed on your device and never transmitted.

## Data Storage

Everything the App creates (stories, search indexes, settings) is stored on your device only. No copy is kept on any server.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. There is no advertising, ad tracking, analytics service or data broker in the App. The only data that goes to a third party is described above: crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics), Android text-recognition usage statistics (Google), and payments (Apple or Google).

## Data Deletion

- Settings > Storage > Clear caches removes cached files.
- Uninstalling the App removes all data it stored on your device.
- Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. To ask us to delete them sooner, email us with the approximate date of the crash.

## Children's Privacy

Solej AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
